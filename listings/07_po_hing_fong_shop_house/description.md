# Po Hing Fong Shop House

**Details:** 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, 1 front porch

**Source:** http://www.sohoprintingpress.com (listing 7 of 9)

## Description

Saunter a few steps past the basketball courts of Po Hing Fong, down the hill leading towards Po Yan Street and Tung Wah Hospital and you will find a row of well preserved shop houses on the steps of Po Hing Fong. Behind one of these facades is one of the closest restorations you will find to a real New York Soho loft in Hong Kong. With ceiling heights, and bookshelves, approaching 16 feet, there is no shortage of drama in this downtown loft design.
