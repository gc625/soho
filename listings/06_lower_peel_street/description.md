# Lower Peel Street

**Details:** 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom, 1 long balcony

**Source:** http://www.sohoprintingpress.com (listing 6 of 9)

## Description

For those who love fresh ingredients from Hong Kong’s Gage Street market this is the home to discover. Just half a block up from the markets is this recently restored classic. Embrace the openness of the vast living room separating the two bedroom spaces or step out onto the open-air balcony to enjoy the sunset over Hong Kong low rise building across the street. Take special note of the classic tiles on the balcony that were salvaged from a wanchai demolition or the well worn antique pine flooring imported from the States.
