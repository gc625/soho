# Ulam Penthouses A and B

**Details:** Two 2 Bedroom, 2 Bathroom, Terrace-Roof

**Source:** http://www.sohoprintingpress.com (listing 9 of 9)

## Description

Between Caine and Bridges Street, just above the YMCA on Bridges Street, lies the pedestrian only Ulam Terrace and our two penthouses flats each with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The building has great light and great views over the low rise Sheung Wan neighborhood and the classic medical museum in the rear of the building. Access is from either walking up from Bridges Street or walking down from Caine Road to enter the building. Step out onto either balcony from the living or master bedroom to have your breakfast in the morning or a glass of wine at the end of the day to relax. The roof offers incredible options to entertain your friends or even cool off in the rain shower.
