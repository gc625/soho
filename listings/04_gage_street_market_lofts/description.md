# Gage Street Market Lofts

**Details:** Seven 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom, 2 w/ roofs,

**Source:** http://www.sohoprintingpress.com (listing 4 of 9)

## Description

From the escalator as you cross Lyndhurst Terrace look to the west and you will see the banner that identifies that building that is home to the seven flats we call Gage Street Market Lofts. You could not be closer to the escalator, the wet markets, your gym, the airport express and possibly even your office. These seven flats are all open kitchen and living area with separate enclosed bedroom/bathroom area. All units in the building have been renovated and now occupied by young professionals who enjoy being in the heart of Central.
