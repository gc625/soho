# Staunton and Aberdeen

**Details:** 2 bedroom 2 bathroom, communal roof

**Source:** http://www.sohoprintingpress.com (listing 1 of 9)

## Description

When your travels next take you from Soho to PMQ along Staunton Street, take a moment to glance up at the 4/F of the Tong Lau that sits at the intersection of Aberdeen Street. You may notice a vast expanse of old style (but newly built) windows that extend across the front and sides of the building giving front row views of PMQ and nothing but sky. This may be one of the biggest and sunniest Tong Lau homes available in Soho and with two king size en-suite bedrooms with walk in closets. Gather your friends for dining and cocktails around the massive granite topped center counter, or go up to the roof to take in the city views.
