## Description

This is our office and base of operations well situated to respond to tenants needs as and when they arise. It is right in the middle of all the Soho Printing Press flats, which extend from Po Yan Street in Sheung Wan to Gage Street in Central. Like all the properties in our portfolio, the Soho Printing Press shop house was renovated to retain as much of the original character as possible while upgrading the facilities to meet the standards of a modern lifestyle.
