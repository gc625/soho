About Us

Old buildings,
carefully re-inked.

Soho Printing Press is a small portfolio of classic Hong Kong homes — Tong Lau apartments and old shop houses — strung along the Central–Mid-Levels escalator, from Po Yan Street in Sheung Wan to Gage Street in the heart of Central.

Every building is restored the same way: keep as much of the original character as possible — salvaged tiles, exposed brick, worn timber — while quietly upgrading everything underneath to the standards of a modern lifestyle.
